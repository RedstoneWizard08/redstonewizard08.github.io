# The Broken Script

## Overview

The Broken Script is an analog horror Minecraft mod, containing several random
events and entities you encounter throughout the game. It takes itself slowly,
ramping up the tension and building a creepy atmosphere over time, without a
true release. It allows real, raw, fear to develop in the player's mind,
un-knowing of what is there to come. It develops an ARG (alternate reality
game), a sort of internet mystery told through small clues left for people to
find. The ARG tells the story of what happened in the mod's deep lore, and why.

## Reach

Currently, it is sitting at over **2 million downloads** on both Modrinth and
CurseForge, and has a large and dedicated community figuring out its secrets and
story.

## Authorship

The mod was originally created by wendigodrip, and since he left I have taken
over core development responsibilities and lead the incredibly talented team. My
responsibilities include writing and optimizing code, managing the team,
overseeing the development of the ARG and lore, and leading the development of
assets.

The mod is written in **Kotlin** and **Java**, for Minecraft 1.21.1, and almost
all code in the mod is loader-agnostic, and could work on both Fabric and
NeoForge.

The mod is closed-source, however its core library that contains the foundations
for almost all features will be open-source upon the release of version 2.0 of
the mod.
